Warranty coverage depends on the applicable vehicle warranty, component, vehicle condition, time, mileage and other relevant requirements. Submission of a claim does not by itself establish warranty coverage.

What Happens Next?

1. Request Received

Your warranty request is recorded with a unique reference number.

2. Review

The submitted vehicle and issue information can be reviewed as part of the warranty process.

3. Service

If service is required, the applicable service process can be arranged.
