Vehicle Agreement

Important information and terms relating to vehicle orders, purchases, deliveries, financing, and ownership.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Important Notice

This page is a frontend demonstration and is not a legally binding vehicle purchase agreement. Before production use, the final agreement should be reviewed, customized, and approved by qualified legal counsel and the appropriate business, financing, delivery, and regulatory teams.

1. Agreement Overview

This Vehicle Agreement describes general terms that may apply when a customer requests, orders, purchases, finances, leases, or takes delivery of a vehicle through this website.

The final terms applicable to a particular transaction may depend on the vehicle, configuration, location, applicable laws, financing provider, delivery method, and documents presented to the customer before purchase.

2. Customer Information

Customers are responsible for providing accurate, complete, and current information during the ordering, financing, registration, insurance, and delivery processes.

Information may include name, contact information, identification information, delivery address, payment information, and other information reasonably required to complete a transaction.

3. Vehicle Configuration

Vehicle availability, specifications, colors, options, equipment, estimated range, performance information, pricing, and delivery estimates may vary by vehicle, configuration, market, and production availability.

Images and specifications displayed on this website may be illustrative. Customers should review the final configuration and applicable transaction documents before completing a purchase.

4. Vehicle Pricing

The price presented during an order may include the selected vehicle configuration and applicable options. Taxes, registration fees, delivery charges, financing costs, insurance requirements, and other applicable charges may be additional.

Prices and incentives may change before an order is finalized, subject to applicable law and the terms shown during the transaction.

5. Deposits and Payments

Certain vehicle transactions may require a deposit, reservation payment, down payment, or other amount before delivery.

Payment requirements, refundability, payment deadlines, and applicable fees should be clearly disclosed in the final transaction documents presented to the customer.

6. Financing and Leasing

Financing and leasing may be subject to separate agreements and approval by an applicable financing or leasing provider.

Estimated payments displayed by this website are for demonstration purposes unless expressly identified as official financing terms.

7. Delivery

Delivery dates and locations may depend on vehicle availability, production schedules, transportation, registration requirements, customer documentation, and other circumstances.

A delivery estimate is not necessarily a guaranteed delivery date unless the applicable final agreement expressly provides otherwise.

8. Inspection and Acceptance

Customers should inspect their vehicle at delivery and promptly identify any apparent damage, missing equipment, or other discrepancies according to the applicable delivery procedures.

Additional inspection, acceptance, and reporting requirements may apply under the final purchase or lease documents.

9. Registration and Insurance

Customers may be responsible for completing applicable registration, titling, licensing, and insurance requirements before or after delivery.

Requirements vary by jurisdiction and may be subject to local government and insurance rules.

10. Warranty

New vehicles may be covered by applicable limited warranties. Warranty coverage, exclusions, duration, mileage limits, and service procedures are determined by the applicable warranty documentation.

Nothing on this demonstration page should be interpreted as replacing an applicable vehicle warranty.

11. Vehicle Use and Maintenance

Customers are responsible for operating and maintaining their vehicle in accordance with applicable instructions, safety requirements, laws, and manufacturer guidance.

Failure to properly maintain or operate a vehicle may affect safety, performance, warranty coverage, or other applicable rights and obligations.

12. Software and Connected Services

Certain vehicle functions may depend on software, connectivity, mobile applications, accounts, or other digital services.

Software features and connected services may change over time and may be subject to separate terms.

13. Cancellation and Returns

Cancellation rights, return policies, deposits, fees, and other applicable remedies may vary according to the transaction, jurisdiction, vehicle type, financing or leasing arrangement, and applicable law.

Customers should review the final transaction documents for the terms applicable to their specific purchase.

14. Limitation of Demonstration Information

Information on this page is provided as a general frontend demonstration. It does not create a purchase obligation, financing commitment, warranty, delivery guarantee, or other legally binding promise.

15. Changes to This Agreement

A production vehicle agreement may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in products, services, applicable law, financing arrangements, operational requirements, or other circumstances.

The version presented to a customer during an applicable transaction should identify the terms governing that transaction.
