Charging basics

A Simple Charging Routine

Follow these general steps when charging your vehicle. Exact equipment, charging speed, and procedures can vary by vehicle and location.

01

Park Safely

Position your vehicle safely near the charging equipment.

02

Connect

Connect the compatible charging equipment to the vehicle.

03

Confirm Charging

Check the vehicle interface or charging equipment to confirm that charging has started.

04

Disconnect

When charging is complete, stop charging if necessary and safely disconnect the connector.
